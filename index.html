ABOUT

A local tutor who understands what school can be like.

I'm Callum, a 16-year-old sixth-form student from Coggeshall. I offer one-to-one Maths and English tuition for students aged 9–16, with lessons designed around the individual student rather than a fixed programme.

I've only recently been through GCSEs myself, so I understand what it is like to sit in the student's position. My aim is to make difficult topics feel less intimidating: explain them clearly, work through examples together and then let the student have a go.

I also have experience supporting younger students as a reading mentor. Parents can contact me directly to discuss what their child is working on and whether I can help.
